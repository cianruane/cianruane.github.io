Cian Ruane

Economist (Manager), Central Bank of Ireland
Research Affiliate, UCL Stone Centre for Inequality

I am an economist in the Irish Economic Analysis division at the Central Bank of Ireland. Prior to this, I spent five years as an economist in the International Monetary Fund’s Research Department and China Division. I am also a Research Affiliate at UCL’s Stone Centre for Inequality.

I received my PhD in Economics from Stanford University in 2018. My research focuses on firms and productivity and implications for the macroeconomics of development.
